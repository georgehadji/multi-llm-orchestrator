Cutting-Edge Innovation for Precise Vision Correction

At Thessaloniki Vision Clinic, we combine world-class ophthalmology with the latest femtosecond laser, OCT imaging, and AI-guided diagnostics to deliver exceptional outcomes for every patient.

Femtosecond Laser

Bladeless, computer-guided corneal incisions with sub-micron precision for LASIK, cataract capsulotomy, and corneal transplants.

OCT Angiography

Non-invasive, high-resolution imaging of retinal and choroidal vasculature — essential for early detection of macular degeneration and diabetic retinopathy.

AI-Powered Diagnostics

Deep learning algorithms analyze retinal scans, visual fields, and corneal topography to flag pathology with 98% sensitivity — supporting our specialists in real time.

3D Surgical Planning

Pre-operative 3D modeling of the anterior segment and retina allows our surgeons to simulate every step before entering the OR — reducing risk and improving accuracy.
