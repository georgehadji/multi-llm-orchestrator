Our Legacy

The Legend of Gadini Barberia

Founded in the historic alleys of Bologna, Italy in 1986 by Master Barber Matteo Gadini, Gadini Barberia was born out of a desire to protect and elevate the timeless rituals of classic Italian barbering.

To us, grooming is not a chore—it is an art form, a therapeutic ritual, and a masculine sanctuary. Our barbers are trained extensively in the classic scissors-over-comb method, hot lather straight-razor neck shaves, and custom beard sculpting designed to accent each individual's facial structure.

We select only the finest premium pomades, botanical beard oils, and sandalwood-infused shave creams to treat your hair and skin. Walk in, pour yourself an espresso or vintage Italian amaro, and relax in our restored 1950s Belmont leather chairs.
